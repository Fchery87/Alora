# 01 — Competitor feature landscape

Type: research
Status: resolved
Blocked by: —

## Question

What features do comparable baby-care apps ship, and where are the gaps Alora could own?

Catalog the comparable apps — Huckleberry, Nara Baby, Baby Tracker, Baby Daybook, Onoco, Sprout Baby, Napper, What to Expect, Cubo Ai, and any other notable ones — and produce a feature matrix covering: logging types (feeds, diapers, sleep, pumping, solids, medicine, milestones), insights/analytics (sleep trends, growth percentiles, patterns), reminders, growth charts, photo journaling, AI features, co-parenting/collaboration (multiple caregivers, invites, roles), community/content, offline/local-first behavior, privacy posture, and pricing model.

Deliverable: the matrix plus 5–10 concrete, source-backed feature ideas for Alora grounded in what comparable apps do and don't do — each with a one-line rationale tied to Alora's two-caregiver local-first angle.

Findings land at `.scratch/launch-readiness/research/01-competitor-feature-landscape.md` (sourced, with URLs).

## Answer

Resolved. Findings: `.scratch/launch-readiness/research/01-competitor-feature-landscape.md` — VERIFIED v2 (web-verified pass applied Aug 2026; v1 was an unverified draft because the researcher role had no web access).

- 13 comparable apps cataloged with a 3-matrix feature landscape (logging breadth, insights/reminders/photos, collaboration/content/offline/privacy/pricing) vs Alora's MVP.
- Key verified corrections vs the draft: Nara Baby is entirely free (no premium tier); Huckleberry Premium is $119.99/yr (SweetSpot + Berry AI + custom sleep plans); Baby Connect is subscription-based (Family $4.99/mo, Professional $14.99/mo), not one-time; Amila Baby Tracker has no automatic multi-device sync (manual export/import); Onoco US $8.99/mo/$59.99/yr with family-extended premium; Sprout – Baby Tracker & Log $7.99 one-time; Glow Premium $59.99/yr (family $89.99/yr); What to Expect and BabyCenter ship free trackers.
- 10 feature ideas for Alora (5 GAP, 5 COPY): shift-handoff briefing card, visible sync/durability trust UX, possible-duplicate merge flow, WHO growth charts, non-clinical wake-window suggestions, pumping + milk-stash inventory, local-first photo attach, limited caregiver seat (grandparent/nanny), privacy-as-UX trust center, pediatrician-visit PDF report.
- Open items for later verification: Cubo Ai regulatory status, Pebbi (unknown competitor named in the repo audit), pricing drift.
