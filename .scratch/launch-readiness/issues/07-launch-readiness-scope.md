# 07 — Launch readiness scope

Type: grilling
Status: resolved
Blocked by: 05

## Question

What does "launch-ready" concretely require after the beta passes its exit criteria?

Decide, with the human: store assets (icon, screenshots, feature graphics), store listing copy (title, subtitle, description, category), the public privacy policy URL (required by both stores), EULA/terms, review processes (what to check before submission), and a release checklist. Scope what this map hands off to the launch effort as a fresh effort.

Resolved when the launch-readiness checklist is written at `.scratch/launch-readiness/launch-checklist.md`, scoping the handoff (and spawning prototype tickets for store assets if the fidelity needs it).

## Answer

Resolved by founder grilling (Aug 2026). Deliverable: `.scratch/launch-readiness/launch-checklist.md` (Status: ready-for-human).

- Store assets: prototype ticket once the beta starts (icon + screenshots from the design system, founder review).
- Listing copy: agent drafts title/subtitle/description from PRD + research; founder reviews.
- Privacy policy + EULA: agent draft from a template tailored to Alora's documented practices; founder review; legal sign-off gates submission (ties to MVP 14 COPPA). EULA via store standards for beta.
- Review processes: full pre-submission checklist (Beta App Review prep with privacy URL + test info, Play Data-safety + adult audience, TestFlight build notes, in-app 5.1.1 link pass).
- Handoff scope: the checklist IS the handoff artifact (release flow, listings, review responses, launch/no-launch decision, announcement); store execution after beta exit criteria = a fresh effort; post-launch ops beyond first-week monitoring excluded.
