# 07 — Launch readiness scope

Type: grilling
Status: open
Blocked by: 05

## Question

What does "launch-ready" concretely require after the beta passes its exit criteria?

Decide, with the human: store assets (icon, screenshots, feature graphics), store listing copy (title, subtitle, description, category), the public privacy policy URL (required by both stores), EULA/terms, review processes (what to check before submission), and a release checklist. Scope what this map hands off to the launch effort as a fresh effort.

Resolved when the launch-readiness checklist is written at `.scratch/launch-readiness/launch-checklist.md`, scoping the handoff (and spawning prototype tickets for store assets if the fidelity needs it).
